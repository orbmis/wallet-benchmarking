Each wallet gets a 0–100 score per category, then is plotted on the two categories you pick for the X and Y axes. Top-right is the magic quadrant: strong on both.

How a category score is computed

  • Every rankable metric in the category scores the wallet on the same better/worse-for-the-user polarity as the matrix: Good = 1, Partial = 0.5, Worse = 0.
  • Counts are normalised against the field's range — more audits scores higher, more open high-severity flaws scores lower.
  • The category score is the average of the scored metrics ×100. — Unknown cells are skipped (never counted as 0), and descriptive Value metrics (licence, default transfer mode) have no order so they're excluded.
  • A wallet with no data for either chosen axis can't be placed — it's listed under the chart.

Quadrant split

The dashed crosshair defaults to the median of the plotted wallets (relative positioning), or switch it to a fixed 50 / 100 line for an absolute read.
